Why it feels different

Instead of memorizing labels, interact with layers, motion, and structural relationships. Move slowly, choose a structure, then connect the model to what you can feel in your own body.

Important boundary

These are simplified 2D illustrations, not clinically validated biomechanics, diagnostic tools, or representations of your exact anatomy. Pain maps and sensory territories overlap. Stop any self-experiment that provokes symptoms.
