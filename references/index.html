Education & provenance

Know what you are seeing.

BODY / LIVE teaches structural relationships using diagrammatic, interactive illustrations. It is not a diagnostic instrument, radiological scan, medical treatment recommendation, validated 3D reconstruction, or biomechanical force model.

How anatomy is represented

The original project illustrations use stylized SVG geometry authored for teaching. Anatomical layers sometimes have intentionally separated or exaggerated spacing so structures remain visible. Joint motion is qualitative. Knee flexion is a sagittal-inspired illustration; shoulder movement demonstrates arm elevation with simplified scapular rotation; hip movement illustrates abduction in a posterior view. Other movement axes are discussed but not simulated as if they were implemented.

Nerve paths and skin sensation territories are approximate. Root distributions and peripheral nerve fields overlap and can vary among people. Neither pain locations nor sensory maps identify the cause of a person's symptoms.

Illustration rights and reuse

The V3 app retains its project-originated SVG drawings and existing cover illustrations. This release does not import unlicensed commercial anatomical assets or copy medical textbook illustrations. Its textual summaries paraphrase general anatomical facts; the reference links support further reading.

Learning through your own body

Gentle awareness prompts are optional. Do not push through pain, force joint range, perform aggressive stretches, or attempt nerve tension tests. Stop if an activity triggers or increases numbness, weakness, or pain. Contact a qualified healthcare professional for persistent or worsening symptoms.
