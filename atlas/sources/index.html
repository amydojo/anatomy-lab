VOL. 06 · PROVENANCE / VALIDITY

What is sourced. What is inferred.

Educational reference atlas, not a medical-grade or patient-specific reconstruction. Qualified anatomical and licensing reviews of the integrated Hip/Nerve work are pending. A linked scene or smooth animation cannot establish biological continuity.

01 / Full-body overview

The overview uses 78 pinned BodyParts3D 4.0 skeletal reference-pose components. It is derived from the pinned atlas with one uniform transform. Simplified mobile assets retain per-part FMA IDs, source hashes and reduction counts in the provenance manifest. BodyParts3D © the Database Center for Life Science, CC BY 4.0. Missing hands, feet, soft tissues and organs mean this is not a complete anatomical body.

02 / Hip and knee

Hip and Knee use source-matched BodyParts3D 4.0 geometry. The verified hip-knee frame transform aligns 480 corresponding femoral vertices within rounding. This proves a skeletal reference correspondence, NOT clinical biomechanics or nerve placement.

03 / The sciatic composite

The Z-Anatomy-derived sciatic, tibial and common-fibular surfaces are separate from the BodyParts3D hip, with CC BY-SA 4.0 derivative license notice. They were registered by automatic femoral ICP; the nerve manifest documents exact inputs, transforms, errors and limitations. Median ~6.2 mm and P95 ~14.3 mm describe femur surface fit, not independently measured sciatic nerve location errors. Shared donor is not verified. The sampled nerve path and branch gaps are estimates, not imaged fascicle or MR neurography reconstructions.

04 / Explicit source seam

The Hip → Knee transition shows a source-boundary message. The Knee model does not contain a continuously reconstructed sciatic nerve. We do not draw imaginary connective nerve tissue through unavailable anatomy.
