# GRAVITY III — stable pivot / free look
Continuation of b4746d7 on GRAVITY branch. No renderer, source geometry or shared bindings changed. gravity=1 defaults to III; gravityVersion=2 restores II, gravityVersion=1 restores I.
## Camera responsibilities
Atlas projection uses renderer reference origin target, inverse view rotation R, virtual eye E=target+R(0,.04,7.4). Zoom is projection scale, not physical dolly. Free roam preserves E during pure look by reconstructing renderer target; camera-relative lateral translation uses current right/up (no forward movement mapping added). Free angular gain scales with projected focal length to avoid huge macro jumps. Inspect mode resolves explicit selected pivot, keeps its view coordinates under rotation and pixel under zoom, then applies pan separately. Offscreen pivots remain valid unless unsafe depth or selection/space is invalid. No implicit recenter, no clipping/collision claims. Translation bounded without snapping; orientation never clamped via target bounds. Existing R3 recovery, Cross selection and Circle clear selection. No new shortcuts.
## NEXUS PLAYGROUND adapter (must be adopted by NEXUS)
At exact hit (controller Cross and pointer selectAt/pick), store ref=BodyGravityAdaptive.pivotCapture(hit.id, spaceId, hit.point, playground.transform(hit.id)); spaceId must include source manifest and region. Resolve on each motion step with pivotResolve(ref, selectedId, spaceId, playground.transform(selectedId)); return {key:spaceId+':'+selectedId,point} through world.orbitPivot(camera). Do not overwrite ref with bounding-box center in playChange; keep original hit in local coordinates. Clear ref on deselect, invalid manifest, source change, incompatible restore. Re-select when no exact hit available. Do not use renderedIds for pivot lifetime (culling isn't deselection). Reset camera motion when PLAYGROUND claims triggers/sticks to avoid prior camera inertia leaking into grasp; call motion.reset at input ownership transition, not every frame. Preserve existing SHARE, grasp, audio and capture actions.
