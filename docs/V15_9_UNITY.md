## Phase 1 — same-source complete available skeleton
Existing 61 pinned meshes include two femoral registration references; 59 are currently rendered. Expected 82 additional source files (143 minus 61) and 84 additional renderable entities (143 minus 59). Acquire all 11 anatomical folders using exact pinned LFS hashes. Reuse existing GLBs byte-for-byte; no rotations, translations, mirroring, or per-bone rescaling. Check laterality by folder/name and relative source X, and proximal-distal order as preliminary screening. Record file counts, original and derivative hashes, bounds, triangles, vertices. Preserve L6 and combined labels. This is the source's available skeleton, not a conventional 206-bone individually segmented skeleton. CT elbow gaps, grouped phalanges/skull/sacrum/sternum and absent hyoid/costal cartilage/sesamoids remain visible limitations; no invented fillers.

## Phase 2 — coordinate and rendering qualification
Retrieve pinned CT NIfTI qform/sform and associated segmentation NRRD headers; verify image orientation, spacing, handedness, units and mesh export convention (including LPS/RAS sign changes). Compare sampled source STL surface positions to corresponding voxel-label boundaries in physical space, at both ends of body. Record residuals with voxel spacing and annotation uncertainty. AABB checks alone cannot establish this. Require immutable manifest and derivative hashes; add whole-body fit, landmark travel and full-body spatial index in a new opt-in source mode after qualified offline LOD streaming. Preserve existing AXIS and Denver modes, exact inspection geometry and independent source identities. Verify complete available skeleton, desktop/mobile screenshots, 143 IDs in manifest, expected in-view draws, missing assets, context loss, memory, controller continuity and physical device acceptance. A global fit must adapt to portrait and landscape; do not simply reuse axial camera fit or assume all 143 should be drawn during close-ups. No cross-source duplicate pelvis/femurs.

## Phase 3 — Denver soft-tissue registration (separate gate)
Previous two-femur rigid ICP held out hips and sacrum+coccyx. Sacral median 3.3399 > 3 and centroid 10.5630 > 5 source units; p95 7.3015 < 8. Prior decision remains blocked. Source documentation confirms BoneHub SACRUM contains coccyx; compare against union of Denver Sacrum and Coccyx without changing fit. Published Denver overclosure adjustment preserves bone when paired with deformable tissue; don't attribute bone mismatch to that step without stage-specific evidence. Denver paper documents offsets between modalities, aligned images, raw and smoothed STLs. Compare raw versus final bones after recovering metadata-derived transform; distinguish unit/axis/origin differences from segmentation and smoothing. Prior raw transform must be composed with Denver storage translation [-620,-430,-500] and respective display transforms, not applied directly to packed display vertices.
Predeclare fit and held-out regions: test image-header-derived rigid mapping; if correction needed use bilateral long-bone/hip references with held-out sacrum+coccyx, patellae, fibulae, ankle/foot anchors and independent landmark points. Use side-specific matched anatomy, no grouped/unmatched structures in fit, no held-out leakage. Report exact point-to-triangle bidirectional median/p95, surface Dice at voxel-aware tolerance, independent landmark TRE, signed joint gap/penetration, attachment consistency, and regional uncertainty. Compare rigid against similarity only after proving units; no per-bone free placement or nonlinear fitting to force pass. Do not lower prior gates. Set physically meaningful acceptance thresholds only after spacing and intended use are verified, independently review them, and preserve failures. If global rigid fails, investigate image-stage registration; if residual tissue mismatch remains, retain Denver as a separate labeled mode. Any future regional deformable registration must be separately validated with bounded displacement and Jacobian/invertibility checks. No clinical or simulation-ready claim.
