## Registration protocol
Historical rigid ICP is BoneHub raw -> Denver raw; do not apply directly to bundle or display coordinates. Sacrum+coccyx held-out median 3.3399 > 3; centroid 10.563021 > 5; physical mm unknown. Preserve failure. Derive image physical transforms from DICOM/NIfTI/NRRD, check handedness/spacing/units and segmentation boundary alignment. Compare header transform and independently fitted rigid candidates; raw versus smoothed versus final Denver bones, union of sacrum+coccyx. Lock fit and held-out annotation sets before optimizing, use repeated independent landmark annotations and uncertainty intervals. Candidate comparator reports separate landmark RMS/max, no auto qualification. No patient or anatomical coordinates are fabricated. Surface validation requires area-weighted exact point-to-triangle bidirectional median/p95, voxel-aware surface Dice and regional error; signed distance only on oriented watertight surfaces, intersection checks with triangle BVH and contact patches. Attachment check requires annotated origin/insertion regions and anatomist review, not nearest bone inference. No fresh contact, attachment, deformation or registration metrics yet. No nonlinear rescue or threshold weakening.

## Rendering/streaming
One selected full-resolution bundle loaded on demand, prior buffers released. 16 MiB compressed/request cap (not GPU or peak process memory cap); SHA and length verified before existing parser. Source-native-only stream contract contains identity, space, LOD and cancellation signal, rejects canonical requests; NANITE adapter not yet wired. Bundle granularity limits selective streaming; next step per-structure qualified LOD assets. Semantic filtering/isolation and opacity operate within loaded bundle. Opacity is simple alpha preview; no anatomical depth hierarchy, multilayer occlusion or order-independent compositing. No physical/controller performance claims. No automatic preload. Browser GPU timings not yet collected.
